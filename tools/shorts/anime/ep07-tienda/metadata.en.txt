Shopping in Spain in 40 Seconds | Real Store Spanish

[description]
Everything you actually say in a Spanish clothing shop, in the order it happens. Browse, try it on, ask for another size, check the sale, decide, pay. 40 seconds.

· Solo estoy mirando. (SO-lo es-TOY mee-RAN-do) I'm just looking.
· ¿Puedo probármelo? (PWEH-do pro-BAR-meh-lo) Can I try it on?
· ¿Tiene otra talla? (TYEH-neh OH-tra TA-ya) Do you have another size?
· ¿Está en rebajas? (es-TA en reh-BA-hass) Is it on sale?
· Me lo llevo. (meh lo YEH-vo) I'll take it.
· ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta? (PWEH-do pa-GAR kon tar-HEH-ta) Can I pay by card?

This is Spain Spanish, not Latin American.
