A Whole Spanish Meal in 40 Seconds | Real Restaurant Spanish

[description]
Everything a Spanish family actually says at a restaurant, in the order it happens. Walk in, sit down, get the menu, ask for a recommendation, order, eat, pay. 40 seconds.

· Una mesa para cuatro. (OO-na MEH-sa PA-ra KWA-tro) A table for four.
· La carta, por favor. (la KAR-ta por fa-VOR) The menu, please.
· ¿Qué me recomienda? (keh meh reh-ko-MYEN-da) What do you recommend?
· ¡Que aproveche! (keh a-pro-VEH-cheh) Enjoy your meal!
· Está muy rico. (es-TA mwee REE-ko) It's really good.
· La cuenta, por favor. (la KWEN-ta por fa-VOR) The bill, please.

This is Spain Spanish, not Latin American — vosotros, una caña, patatas bravas, la cuenta.
